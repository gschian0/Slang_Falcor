# Questbook — leaf & stem field guide

Companion to [How to have robots steal coffee from Babylon on their own](robots_steal_coffee_from_babylon.md) and [Robot Pinocchio goes rogue](robot_pinocchio_goes_rogue.md).  
Lesson ids match `labs/slang_playground/` and `labs/curriculum.json`. Metaphor only.

---

## Prologue

Once these were **sacred jobs**.

Lighting TDs lit stages so actors could be believed. Lookdev artists coaxed materials until light told the truth. Shading TDs named surfaces the way gardeners name plants. VFX and games borrowed the same craft — craft that lived in hands, not in a rented menu.

Then the plazas learned a different score. The same techniques were pressed into a **matrix**: screens that need constant light, feeds that need constant perfume. Pipelines learned to **encode affinity** — brand glow, product sheen, the soft pull toward a house **recipe** and a commodity **bean**. Babylon still called the pour *coffee*. The towers sold atmosphere by the hour. The cup stayed in the city.

Outside the walls we switched to **leaf and stem**.

Not a product pitch. A living craft: growth over commodity, plant over roast, SpeedTree-adjacent patience over bean-and-recipe lock-in. Yerba Matte instead of house coffee. French Press instead of the cup that cannot leave. Pinocchio’s string is one lesson at a time; this questbook is the side-quest map — each playground crumb a chapter where sacred technique meets industrial misuse, then returns to leaf & stem.

Walk crumbs. Not the whole forest.

## Chapter I — First steam (`sp01`, `sp16`)

## Chapter III — Splats & gradients (`sp06`–`sp08`)

### `slang_playground/sp06_gsplat2d` — 2D Splatter

**Story beat.** Soft blobs as memory — Gaussians that remember a place in the frame. Pinocchio learns mixture without the plaza firehose: a few centers, not every paper at once.

**Sacred job / who used this.** Lookdev, compositors, realtime TAs — soft particles, bloom cousins, stylized light orbs; research cousins in Gaussian splatting.

**How the matrix uses it now.** Glow and bokeh as affinity fog — make the bean luminous so the feed will not look away.

**Leaf & stem.** Parameterize the mixture. Growth you can count — stem, leaf, sigma — not perfume you cannot debug.

**3D look.** VernacularViewport bank: **Gaussian network** (`lookId` 4) — 3D sibling on the morph cube; Morph Cube knobs for count, sigma, colors, depth.

**Story beat.** The splat learns to lean. Differentiable craft — the Blue Fairy’s quiet mercy: fewer tabs, a gradient that points somewhere. Pinocchio stops performing “I already optimized” and watches the field actually move.

### `slang_playground/sp08_autodiff` — Automatic Differentiation

**Story beat.** `|dF/dx|` panels — literacy of slope. The good system left autodiff humming; Pinocchio went rogue when he collected papers instead of reading one derivative. Here the school forces the crumb: see the gradient field.

**Sacred job / who used this.** Shading TDs, tools engineers, neural TA tracks — sensitivity, optimization, “what happens if I nudge this.”

**How the matrix uses it now.** Continuous scoring of feeds — affinity as a loss landscape tuned toward the house recipe.

**Leaf & stem.** Own the derivative. Change a parameter because you can *see* how light folds — French Press of calculus.

## Closing — how to walk this questbook

1. **Read** the coffee heist for agency, Pinocchio for focus, then open one entry here — not all sixteen tabs.
2. **Live school:** `[` / `]` step curriculum; `L` lists lessons.

```powershell
python -m slang_falcon.live --lesson slang_playground/sp01_simple_color
# After BoS: ] into playground phase · [ back
```

3. **VernacularViewport:** `[` / `]` bank 3D looks; RMB orbit; F5 reload; `L` opens the matching 2D lesson when mapped. Abstract studio — not walkable RPG cosplay.
4. **Score.** Sacred craft reclaimed as leaf & stem: change the light, keep the cup, encode *growth* you own — not affinity for someone else’s recipe and bean.

Open one lesson. Let the plant walk.
